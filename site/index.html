Three steps. No map-checking.

Designed for running somewhere new without getting lost or breaking your stride.

  1. Pick a route

    Browse routes from Richmond Park to the Olympic Park, or draw your own on the map.

  2. Press start

    Phone in your pocket, headphones in. JustinGo follows your GPS along the route.

  3. Follow the voice

    Hear every turn at 200 metres and again as you reach it. Drift off route and it tells you.

Built for running, not driving

Directions that follow park paths, towpaths and trails, where car sat-navs can't go.

Turn-by-turn voice

A heads-up 200 metres out, a reminder at the turn, and back-to-back turns announced together.

Off-route alerts

Stray more than 30 metres from the route and you'll hear it straight away, then a confirmation when you're back.

Route creator

Tap points on the map and the route snaps to real paths. Add your own voice messages anywhere along it.

Pace and splits

Time, distance, current pace and estimated finish while you run. Kilometre splits when you're done.

Local stories

Routes talk about what you're passing, from the Serpentine to the Prime Meridian.

GPX in and out

Bring in a route from your watch or another app, or take a JustinGo route anywhere.

Across Greater London

Every route checked and voiced, from a quick 3 km loop to nearly 19 km round Richmond Park.

Plus any route you draw yourself.
