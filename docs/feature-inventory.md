# Feature inventory and business models

What a RunGo-class product offers, researched from rungoapp.com, routes.rungoapp.com and both app
store listings on 2026-09-17, and where each item stands in JustinGo.

Status key:
- **Built**: works in the app today.
- **Demo**: on screen with placeholder images and unwired buttons, so the full product can be shown.
- **Later**: planned, not on screen yet.

Tiers are JustinGo's proposed tiers (below), not RunGo's. JustinGo is free during the preview.

## Plans (proposed, shown in the demo)

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| Free | £0 | Everyone: voice navigation, all routes, create routes, run log |
| Premium | £4.99 / month, £44.99 / year, £99 lifetime | Runners who want more maps, offline extras, live tracking, Strava |
| Creator | £16.99 / month, £169 / year (includes Premium) | Clubs, coaches, races and route publishers |

## Business models

| Model | Who pays | What they get | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consumer subscriptions | Runners | Premium and Creator plans. No ads | Demo |
| Hotels and hospitality | Hotels, hotel groups | Voice-guided routes from the lobby, staff-narrated walks, brand page, QR cards, concierge mapping tool, digital hotel guide, listing in the hotel finder, usage analytics. Starter trial then annual programme | Demo |
| Races and events | Race directors, charities | Course maps and embeds, audio course guidance with sponsor messages, live tracking for spectators, leaderboards, virtual races, badges, GPS timing, participant how-tos. Self-serve free, paid setup | Demo |
| Tourism and destinations | Tourism boards, councils | City guides, themed trails and audio tours, challenge series | Demo |
| Corporate wellness | Employers | Team challenges, virtual races, leaderboards | Demo |
| Brands and sponsors | Brands (usually via races and hotels) | Audio activations, branded routes, share images and badges | Demo |
| Clubs, coaches, publishers | Clubs, coaches | Creator plan, branded groups | Demo |
