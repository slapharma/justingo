  2. Press start

    Phone in your pocket, headphones in. JustinGo follows your GPS along the route.

    Hear every turn at 200 metres and again as you reach it. Drift off route and it tells you.

Questions

Is JustinGo free?

Yes. Everything is free during the preview. After that, voice navigation, every route and route creation stay free, and Premium and Creator add extras for runners, clubs and races who want more.

Which phones does it work on?

Right now it runs in the browser on iPhone and Android, so you can try it today without installing anything. Dedicated iPhone and Android apps are coming soon, with Apple Watch to follow.

Does it work offline?

Download for offline is on the way and will be free. In the preview, open your route while you have signal before you set off.

Can I make my own routes?

Yes. Tap points on the map and the route snaps to real paths, then add your own voice messages anywhere along it. You can also import a GPX file. Free includes a monthly number of routes, Premium makes it unlimited.

How do hotels and races get started?

Book a demo and we'll map your first routes with you. Hotels begin with a starter trial, then move to an annual programme. Races can set up for free themselves, or have us do the setup.

What happens to my location?

Your location is used to guide you along the route. In the preview, your routes and run history are stored on your own device. Live tracking will only share your position when you switch it on and send the link.
